A real-time escape race for 3-5 players, set in ORPHEON, an abandoned research station. Everyone plays at once for 10 minutes: roll your symbol dice, open sectors, fight unknown creatures and collect treasure. Round 1 collects points; in round 2 the station collapses and you have to escape. Only those who escape keep their points.

[h2]How to start[/h2]
Sit at Red, Yellow, Green, Blue or Purple. Your mat is on your side of the table: its top shows what each action needs, its right side the treasure points. Press START GAME (top left): it shuffles the decks, hands out the dice, puts everyone in the Safe Zone and starts the 10-minute timer. ROLL DICE rolls your 4 dice. RESET TABLE puts everything back.

[h2]Rules[/h2]
[list]
[*]Play in cycles: everyone rolls 4 dice, keeps some and rerolls the rest once, acts, then waits for the others.
[*]2 Movement: move to a connected room, or open a new one (round 1 only).
[*]Monsters have 1, 2 or 3 HP (Scraper, Carrier, Anomaly) and give as many points. Each Attack deals 1 damage and the damage stays: right-click the monster, Hit.
[*]2 Magic: take a treasure (Module, Archive, Specimen) or a special object. Treasure scores alone or in combinations: see your mat.
[*]At 5:00 round 2 starts with Rockfall: rocks block every Unregistered Sector with nobody in it and no new rooms can be opened.
[*]In round 2, place a special object on the Integration Chamber. That is your special task.
[*]At 0:00, only players in the Safe Zone who completed the task keep their points. Most points wins.
[/list]
