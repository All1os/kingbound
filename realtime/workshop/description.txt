A real-time dungeon race for 3-5 players. Everyone plays at once for 10 minutes: roll your symbol dice, open rooms, beat monsters and grab treasure. Round 1 collects points; in round 2 an event threatens the dungeon and you escape to the Safe Room with what you carry. Most points wins.

[h2]How to start[/h2]
Sit at Red, Yellow, Green, Blue or Purple. Your mat is on your side of the table, and its top shows what each action needs. Press START GAME (top left): it shuffles the decks, hands out the dice, puts everyone in the Safe Room and starts the 10-minute timer. ROLL DICE rolls your 4 dice. RESET TABLE puts everything back.

[h2]Rules[/h2]
[list]
[*]Roll your 4 dice whenever you like. Keep the ones you need and reroll the rest, as often as you want.
[*]2 Movement: open a new room (take the top card of the room deck, flip it and lay it next to your room with the passages connected) or move to a connected room.
[*]Monsters need 2, 3 or 4 Attack (weak, medium, strong). A beaten monster goes to POINTS on your mat.
[*]Treasure needs 2 Magic and goes to CARRIED. Bring it back to the Safe Room to move it to POINTS. A treasure alone gives nothing: one common, one rare and one special make a set worth 5.
[*]Three special objects hide among the treasure. Place one in the special objects room for 2 points.
[*]At 5:00 round 2 starts with an event card: rooms close, or a strong monster wakes up and chases you.
[*]When the timer ends, treasure still CARRIED is lost and only POINTS count. Tie: most monsters beaten wins.
[/list]
