A real-time dungeon race for 3-5 players. Everyone plays at once for 10 minutes: roll your symbol dice, open rooms, beat monsters and grab treasure. Treasure only counts once you carry it back to the Safe Room. Most points wins.

[h2]How to start[/h2]
Sit at Red, Yellow, Green, Blue or Purple. Your mat is on your side of the table, and its top shows what each action needs. Press START GAME (top left): it shuffles the decks, hands out the dice, puts everyone in the Safe Room and starts the 10-minute timer. RESET TABLE puts everything back.

[h2]Rules[/h2]
[list]
[*]Roll your 4 dice whenever you like. Keep the ones you need and reroll the rest, as often as you want.
[*]2 Movement: open a new room (take the top card of the room deck, flip it and lay it next to your room with the passages connected) or move to a connected room.
[*]Monsters need 2, 3 or 4 Attack (weak, medium, strong). A beaten monster goes to POINTS on your mat.
[*]Treasure needs 2 Magic and goes to CARRIED. Bring it back to the Safe Room to move it to POINTS, no dice needed.
[*]Traps go off when you walk in: Spike Pit, Pressure Plate, Collapsing Passage.
[*]Loot cards help you. Prank cards hit other players, but never someone in the Safe Room.
[*]When the timer ends, only POINTS count. Tie: most monsters beaten wins.
[/list]
